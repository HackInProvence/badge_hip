CTF Panic
The cicadas of Hack In Provence

// A gamebook for the SecSea badge, in La Ciotat.
// Format: see docs/en/gamebooks.md. Check: python tools/gamebook_check.py this_file.txt
// French version: panique_ctf.txt (same section numbers).
// Items: USB key and log (server room), navettes (coffee), map (Jojo), Flipper (Lilou),
// lockpicks (Bernard), signal (Marius's remote), proof (the robot in the shipyard).

== 1
It's the day of the SecSea CTF final, in La Ciotat. You are still asleep when your cicada badge buzzes:

"ALERT: scoreboard hacked!"

You rush to the conference. On the big screen, every team has zero points. At the top, an ASCII art cicada with 31337 points sings "CHIRP-CHIRP".

Mireille, head of the CTF, grabs your sleeve.
- No culprit, no final tonight! Your team is still in bed? Then you investigate!

-> 2 : Run to the server room
-> 3 : Look around the main hall
-> 4 : Coffee first, it's vital

== 2
The server room is a closet behind the stage. Jojo, the sysadmin, has red eyes and his t-shirt on inside out.
- The scores were rewritten last night at 3:12 am, from the conference wifi. But the hall was locked!

Near the rack, something shiny lies on the floor: a USB key with a cicada sticker.

-> 5 [!USB key] : Pick up the USB key
-> 6 : Question Jojo
-> 3 : Go to the main hall

== 3
The hall is buzzing. Stands everywhere, stickers everywhere, cables everywhere. You hear soldering irons, clicking padlocks and a speaker testing a mic.

On the giant screen, the ASCII cicada is still singing. The final starts tonight at 8 pm.

-> 10 : The hardware village
-> 15 : The lockpicking table
-> 19 : The talk room
-> 22 : The sponsor stand
-> 25 : Go out to the beach
-> 29 : Go to the old shipyard
-> 50 : Report to Mireille

== 4
The coffee line is long. In front of you, a tall guy in a black hoodie whispers on the phone:
- Tonight I wipe everything. Nobody will ever know what was there.

He takes his coffee and heads for the beach. On the table sits a box of navettes, the orange blossom biscuits of Provence. You grab a handful.

+[navettes]

-> 23 : Follow the black hoodie
-> 2 : Go to the server room
-> 3 : Go to the main hall

== 5
You pick up the key. Jojo raises his hands.
- Not mine. And whatever you do, don't plug it in anywhere! A key found on the floor is the oldest trick in the book.

+[USB key]

-> 7 : Plug it into your laptop, quick
-> 8 : Ask for Jojo's quarantine PC
-> 3 : Keep it and go to the hall

== 6
Jojo yawns.
- At night the hall is locked. But the conference wifi reaches the old shipyard, through a radio link on the roof. Last year the CTF had rooms over there.

His stomach growls.
- Sorry. Haven't eaten since yesterday.

-> 9 [navettes] : Offer him your navettes
-> 5 [!USB key] : Pick up the USB key
-> 8 [USB key] : Show him the USB key
-> 3 : Go to the main hall

== 7 FIN perdu
You plug in the key. The screen flickers, a window opens by itself and letters fly across it: a "BadUSB" key, typing on the keyboard for you!

Ten seconds later, your laptop has posted on the CTF chat: "I hacked the scoreboard. CHIRP-CHIRP!"

Mireille puts you on the suspect bench for the day.

Moral: never plug in a key you found on the floor.

== 8
Jojo pulls out an old laptop with no wifi and no hard drive, kept for dodgy objects. The key can't harm anything there.

Inside: a script called "cigalebot.py", a log, and a blurry photo of a rusty electrical cabinet: "SHIPYARD - CABINET 3".

The log says: "Waiting for 433.92 MHz signal... Signal received! Scoreboard updated."

+[log]

-> 29 : Hurry to the old shipyard
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 9
Jojo wolfs down the navettes in three seconds.
- You saved my life. Here, take this.

He hands you a map of the old shipyard, printed last year. A service door is circled in marker: "staff entrance".

-[navettes] +[map]

-> 29 : Go to the shipyard
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 10
The hardware village smells of solder and cold coffee. Lilou, twelve years old, is coding on a Flipper Zero: a small orange gadget with a dolphin on its screen.
- It reads remotes, badges, gates... I'll lend it to you if you finish my LED kit. My iron is too heavy for me.

-> 11 [!Flipper] : Pick up the soldering iron
-> 14 : Ask if she caught anything weird
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 11
You pick up the soldering iron. A drop of solder, one LED leg, then another... You need a steady hand.

The badge rolls the die...

-> 12 [de 1-2] : Hmm, some smoke...
-> 13 [de 3-6] : The LEDs light up!

== 12
The LED is soldered backwards. The kit now blinks "SOS" in Morse. Lilou bursts out laughing.
- That's almost better. But I'm keeping my Flipper for now. Try again?

-> 11 : Pick up the iron again
-> 14 : Talk about weird signals instead
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 13
All the LEDs light up, in the shape of a cicada. Lilou claps.
- A deal is a deal! Here, but give it back tonight.

She shows you: the "Sub-GHz" menu, then "Read", and the Flipper records the radio signals around you.

+[Flipper]

-> 14 : Ask if she caught anything weird
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 14
- Weird? Last night I was camping near the beach with my parents. At 3:12 am my Flipper caught a 433.92 MHz signal. Really strong, right next to the shipyard.

She shrugs.
- Probably a gate remote. They're everywhere around here.

-> 25 : Check out the beach
-> 10 : Back to Lilou's workbench
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 15
Behind the lockpicking table, Bernard, a retired locksmith with a musketeer moustache, lines up see-through padlocks.
- Rule number one: you only pick your own locks, or with the owner's permission. Rule number two: patience.

-> 16 [!lockpicks] : Try a practice padlock
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 16
You slide in the tension wrench, then the pick. You feel the pins, one by one. Bernard holds his breath, and so does his moustache.

The badge rolls the die...

-> 17 [de 1-2] : Crack...
-> 18 [de 3-6] : Click!

== 17
The pick slips, the padlock stays shut, and you pinch your finger.
- Patience, little cicada, says Bernard. It took me twenty years.

-> 16 : Try again
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 18
Click! The padlock opens. Bernard wipes away a tear.
- Well done! Here, a small set of lockpicks. And remember rule number one.

He adds:
- At the old shipyard, Fernand the caretaker lost his key in 1987. He'll be glad to see you.

+[lockpicks]

-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 19
In the talk room, a speaker in a flowery shirt presents "433 MHz: your gates are betraying you".

The room is cool, the seats are soft, the air conditioning purrs...

-> 20 : Ask a question at the end
-> 21 : Close your eyes, just five minutes
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 20
- Many remotes always send the same code, the speaker explains. And cheap models often leave the factory with the same default code!

She adds:
- Never replay a recorded code at random. You don't know what it opens... or where.

-> 25 : Go think on the beach
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 21 FIN perdu
Five minutes turn into five hours. You wake up in the empty room, with the seat pattern printed on your cheek.

Outside, everyone is heading for drinks: with no culprit, the final has been cancelled.

Moral: the air conditioning of a talk room is more dangerous than any hacker.

== 22
At the CyberShield 3000 stand, a salesman in a shiny suit jumps on you.
- Our AI has already found the hacker: the tall guy in the black hoodie! Hackers always wear black hoodies. It's statistics.

He hands you a t-shirt and a forty-page brochure.

-> 23 : Look for the black hoodie
-> 3 : Politely go back to the hall

== 23
The black hoodie walks to the beach, coffee in hand. He stops under a palm tree and talks to himself, waving his arms.
- ...and then I delete everything! All gone!

-> 24 : Walk up and talk to him
-> 42 : Spy on him from behind a palm
-> 3 : Forget it

== 24
- Me, a hacker? He laughs. I'm Zed, I give the closing talk: "How I cleaned up my grandma's PC". I'm rehearsing, that's all!

He thinks for a second.
- But last night, from my hotel, I saw a little light blinking in the old shipyard. Around 3 am.

-> 29 : Go to the shipyard
-> 25 : Stay on the beach
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 25
The beach of La Ciotat sparkles in the sun. At the end of the harbour stand the cranes of the old shipyard.

A fisherman mends a net on his pointu, a little blue wooden boat. A gabian, the huge seagull of Provence, stares at you with a yellow eye. Someone laughs on the bar terrace.

-> 26 : Talk to the fisherman
-> 46 : Approach the seagull
-> 61 : Go to the terrace
-> 66 : Listen to the radio with the badge
-> 28 : Jump into the sea
-> 29 : Go to the shipyard
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 26
- Marius, at your service!

He shows you a brand new little remote.
- I keep my pointu in the old shipyard dock. Every night at 3:12 I open the gate with this, and off I go fishing! Bought it online last week. Two euros!

-> 27 [Flipper] : Record his remote
-> 63 : Ask him to open the gate
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 27
With Marius's permission, you start the Flipper and he presses his remote. The screen shows: "433.92 MHz, fixed code 5A3C". Recorded!

+[signal]

- Fan de chichoune, you can copy these things? Well, if it's for the shipyard gate, you have my permission. Tell Fernand.

-> 43 : Test the code right now
-> 29 : Go to the shipyard
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 28 FIN perdu
The water is 24 degrees, crystal clear, perfect. You float on your back. Then a bit of snorkelling. Then a little nap on the sand.

By evening you have a badge-shaped sunburn, and the final has been cancelled.

Moral: the Mediterranean is a detective's worst enemy.

== 29
The old shipyard is huge: rusty hangars, a giant crane, seagulls everywhere. On a roof, a small antenna points at the conference: the wifi link!

The big gate is shut. Next to it, a small door with an old padlock. In a booth, a caretaker is snoring.

-> 30 : Wake up the caretaker
-> 35 [map] : Find the staff entrance
-> 34 : Climb over the fence
-> 25 : Go to the beach
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 30
- Fernand, caretaker since 1974. The gate? Only Marius opens it, with his remote. And the small door, I lost the key in 1987.

He sighs.
- If you can open it, you have my permission. Me, I use a hole in the fence... but that's a secret.

-> 31 [lockpicks] : Pick the small door
-> 33 [signal] : Replay Marius's code
-> 62 [navettes] : Share your navettes
-> 25 : Fetch Marius from the beach
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 31
Fernand watches you, arms crossed. The padlock is old, rusty and stubborn. Tension wrench, pick, one pin after another...

The badge rolls the die...

-> 32 [de 1] : Crack!
-> 36 [de 2-6] : Click!

== 32
Crack! The pick snaps clean inside the padlock. Fernand nods.
- And that's why I use the fence.

Your lockpicks are done for.

-[lockpicks]

-> 30 : Think of something else with Fernand
-> 3 : Back to the main hall

== 33
- It's Marius's code, and I have his permission, you tell Fernand.

You press the Flipper button. The big gate creaks and swings open.

At that very moment your badge buzzes: "ALERT: scoreboard hacked!" Jojo had only just fixed it...

The gate and the hacker obey the same code. Interesting.

-> 36 : Enter the shipyard

== 34 FIN perdu
You climb the fence. At the top, your t-shirt catches on a spike, and there you stay, between sky and fence, waving your arms.

Firefighters arrive, then tourists, then cameras. By evening the video "The fence cicada" has gone round the whole conference. The final, however, is cancelled.

Moral: you walk around a fence, you don't climb it.

== 35
The map leads you behind hangar 2, along old rails. The service door isn't even locked: just stuck with a paint bucket.

A faded sign: "CTF 2025 staff entrance".

-> 36 : Go in

== 36
The hangar is huge and silent. Tables from last year, stacked chairs, a reel of network cable. At the back, a row of electrical cabinets.

On the third one, a small green LED is blinking. Your badge picks up faint radio noise.

-> 37 : Open cabinet 3
-> 29 : Go back out, it's too quiet

== 37
In the cabinet, between the circuit breakers, a Raspberry Pi is taped to the wall, with a small antenna and a 433 MHz receiver. A sticker: "CIGALE-BOT".

It runs on the shipyard's power and is connected to the conference wifi. It's waiting.

-> 38 : Unplug it right now
-> 39 : Examine it first

== 38
You pull the cable. The LED goes out. No more robot, no more hack!

But when Mireille asks "Who? How? Why?", what will you answer?

-> 39 : Examine it, now it's asleep
-> 59 : Run and tell the good news

== 39
On the case, a handwritten label: "Prank for the 2025 closing. Code 5A3C: the cicada wins the CTF! Lea."

A joke from last year that never worked... and was forgotten in the cabinet. You take photos and keep the label.

+[proof]

But who sends the code every night?

-> 40 [signal] : Compare with Marius's code
-> 41 [log] : Compare with the key's log
-> 64 : Bring the robot to Mireille

== 40
5A3C. The code on the label is exactly the code of Marius's remote!

It all makes sense: Lea used a two-euro remote. Marius bought the same model this week, with the same factory code. Every night at 3:12, on his way fishing, he hacks the CTF without knowing it!

And the robot still gets onto the wifi, because its password hasn't changed since last year.

-> 64 : Bring the robot to Mireille

== 41
The log on the USB key and the robot's log match: "Signal received, 3:12". The key is a backup of CIGALE-BOT, lost yesterday in the server room.

Now, who sends that signal at 3:12? An early riser with a remote?

-> 40 [signal] : Compare with Marius's code
-> 64 : Bring the robot to Mireille

== 42
You hide behind a palm tree. Very discreetly. Well, almost: a seagull lands on your head and screams.

The black hoodie turns around and stares at you.
- Er... Do you want an autograph?

-> 24 : Come out from behind the palm

== 43
You press "Send". Down the boulevard, a garage door opens. Then two. Then five. A man in a vest runs out, shouting.

Your badge buzzes: "ALERT: scoreboard hacked!"

A police officer on a bike rolls up. The badge rolls the die...

-> 44 [de 1-3] : The officer looks angry
-> 45 [de 4-6] : The officer looks curious

== 44 FIN perdu
- Replaying a code that isn't yours, on gates that aren't yours? Station, now!

You spend the afternoon explaining what a Flipper Zero is, then why it has a dolphin. Lilou has to come and pick up her Flipper with her parents. The final is cancelled.

Moral: never replay a recorded code at random. The speaker did warn you.

== 45
The officer listens to your story, then pulls out his own gate remote.
- Two euros, online. Same code?

Yes. He sighs, makes you promise never to replay anything without permission, and pedals away.

This code opens gates... and hacks the scoreboard. The culprit is not far away.

-> 29 : Rush to the shipyard
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 46
The seagull waddles closer. It looks at your badge. It looks at your badge again. Seagulls love shiny things.

-> 48 [navettes] : Offer it a navette
-> 47 : Shoo it away, flapping your arms
-> 25 : Back away slowly

== 47 FIN perdu
You flap your arms. So does the seagull, but it has wings. It grabs your badge by the lanyard and flies off towards the Ile Verte, out at sea.

No badge, no messages, no radio, no investigation. That evening, people say a seagull won the CTF.

Moral: facing a seagull, a biscuit beats a flap of the arms.

== 48
The seagull crunches the navette, thinks it over, then spits something shiny at your feet: an old, well-chewed staff badge.

"Lea - CTF Staff 2025".

It must have found it near the shipyard. Who is this Lea?

-[navettes]

-> 61 : Ask on the terrace
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 49
- Lea, that's me! Last year's organiser. I'm on holiday this year.

When you mention the ASCII cicada, she turns pale.
- Oh no. CIGALE-BOT! My prank for last year's closing. I hid it in a cabinet at the shipyard. It never worked, so I forgot all about it...

-> 29 : Run to the shipyard
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 50
Mireille is waiting in her office, between two boxes of t-shirts.
- So? Who hacked my scoreboard?

-> 51 : The black hoodie, obviously
-> 52 : The sponsor, to sell its AI
-> 65 [!proof] : Lea, last year's organiser
-> 53 [proof] : A forgotten robot at the shipyard
-> 54 [!proof] : A robot... somewhere
-> 3 : I'm not done yet

== 51 FIN perdu
You accuse Zed in front of the whole conference. Silence. Zed spent the night at his hotel, with three witnesses and his grandma on the phone.

The CyberShield 3000 salesman takes a screenshot for his advert. With no culprit, the final is cancelled, and you spend the evening apologising.

Moral: a black hoodie doesn't make a hacker.

== 52 FIN perdu
You accuse CyberShield 3000. The offended salesman packs up his t-shirts and cancels the buffet he was offering tonight.

With no culprit, the final is cancelled. And with no buffet, there's nothing left to eat either.

Moral: you can hate sales brochures without accusing people.

== 53
You put CIGALE-BOT on the desk, with Lea's label. Mireille reads it, reads it again, then bursts out laughing.
- A prank from last year! Jojo will restore yesterday's scores, and we'll finally change the wifi password.

She looks at you.
- And for the final, any idea for a show?

-> 55 : No, just start the final
-> 56 [signal] : A live demo!

== 54
- A robot? Mireille frowns. I can't announce that without proof. Bring me something real!

-> 3 : Go back to investigating

== 55 FIN gagne
At 8 pm the final starts with the real scores. Your team fights like seagulls over a chip and finishes fourth.

At the prize giving, Mireille calls you on stage: special "CTF Sherlock" award, a bronze cicada holding a magnifying glass.

CIGALE-BOT, unplugged, sits at the front desk with a sign: "Change your passwords".

Well done!

== 56
Tonight, in front of the whole conference, you will show how the scoreboard was hacked.

Marius is on stage, very proud, remote in hand. Lilou holds the Flipper. Lea, red as a tomato, has plugged CIGALE-BOT into a test scoreboard.

The badge rolls the die...

-> 57 [de 1-2] : The Flipper is sulking...
-> 58 [de 3-6] : It works first time!

== 57
The Flipper shows "Low battery" and switches off. Awkward silence in the room. Someone coughs.

Marius stands up.
- No problem, I have the original!

He presses his two-euro remote.

-> 58 : Look at the giant screen

== 58 FIN gagne
On the giant screen, the test scoreboard empties and an ASCII cicada appears: "CHIRP-CHIRP!" The room roars with laughter and applause.

You explain everything: a forgotten robot, a wifi password never changed, and two-euro remotes that all share the same code.

Mireille gives you the "CTF Sherlock" award. Lilou wins a brand new soldering iron, Marius a rolling code remote.

Moral: the best hack is the one you explain. Well done!

== 59 FIN
- It's fixed! you shout as you burst into Mireille's office. No more hacking!
- Fixed how? By whom? Why?

You have no idea. Mireille won't take any risk: the final is played on a paper scoreboard, with a marker.

Better than nothing, but the mystery remains.

Moral: fixing is good. Understanding is better.

== 60 FIN
Panisses, olives, lemon syrup: the drinks are lovely. You tell everyone about your investigation, adding a little more suspense each time.

In the evening, the final is replaced by a petanque contest. Your team finishes third.

No culprit today, but a beautiful sunset over the Bec de l'Aigle. The mystery will wait until next year.

== 61
On the terrace they serve lemon syrup and panisses, chickpea fries. A woman in an old "CTF Staff 2025" t-shirt is doing a crossword.

Further along, a group is already raising glasses:
- Cheers! The investigation can wait!

-> 49 : Talk to the woman in the staff t-shirt
-> 60 : Join the drinks
-> 25 : Back to the beach

== 62
Fernand bites into a navette and closes his eyes with delight.
- Just like my mother's... Right. Follow me, but you didn't see anything.

He leads you to a hole in the fence, hidden behind an oleander.

-[navettes]

-> 36 : Squeeze into the shipyard

== 63
- The gate? No problem, let's go!

Marius walks you to the shipyard and presses his remote. The big gate creaks and swings open.

At that very moment your badge buzzes: "ALERT: scoreboard hacked!"

Marius scratches his head.
- Is it the tide doing that?

-> 36 : Enter the shipyard

== 64
You slip CIGALE-BOT, safely unplugged, into your bag and run all the way to the conference. Fernand tips his cap as you pass.

-> 50 : Go and see Mireille

== 65 FIN perdu
- Lea? Mireille calls her at once. Lea swears she hacked nothing last night: she was singing karaoke in Marseille, in front of two hundred witnesses.

Without proof, there's nothing more to say. The final is cancelled, and Lea sulks at you all evening.

Moral: accuse with proof, not with hunches.

== 66
You start the badge's 433 MHz receiver. A weather station says 31 degrees, a car locks itself, a wireless doorbell rings...

Nothing suspicious. What if the hacker's signal only shows up at night? Someone around here may get up very early.

-> 25 : Back to the beach
