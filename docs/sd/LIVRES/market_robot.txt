The Market Robot
The cicadas of Hack In Provence

== 1
Saturday, nine o'clock. On the square of Sainte-Cigalade, the market smells of melons, lavender and roast chicken. The cicadas are already singing in the plane trees.

You stroll along, your cicada badge round your neck and your trusty screwdriver in your pocket, when a cry rings across the square:

- Good heavens! The robot has gone nuts!

Olive, the market's new helper robot, is selling lavender to the fishmonger while singing "chirr-chirr-chirr". And at noon, the mayor gives his speech right in front of it.

On its back: a hatch held by four screws, a big red button, and a bunch of lavender taped on in a rather odd way.

== 5
You push the lavender aside. Underneath is a little black and white square full of pixels: a QR code!

A QR code is text stored as tiny squares, which a phone can read in the blink of an eye. Someone hid this one here on purpose.

Trouble is, your badge has a screen, a radio, LEDs... but no camera. And your phone's battery is flat, of course.

== 7 FIN perdu
You press it. Olive freezes, its screen shows "FARANDOLE MODE", and its loudspeaker bursts into a wild tambourine tune.

It grabs your hand. The fishmonger grabs your other hand, the baker joins in, then the whole market. The dancing line snakes through the village, the fields, the next village...

You get home at nightfall with aching feet. The mayor's speech? Nobody heard it.

Moral: never press a big red button without reading the label.

== 16 FIN perdu
You pull the red wire. Olive gives a deep "beep" and its screen shows "REVERSE MODE"...

You saved the market. But there may be an even better ending...

== 46 FIN

Not quite what was planned... but everyone looks happy.
